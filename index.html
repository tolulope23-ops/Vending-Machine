How It Works

Follow these simple steps to purchase a product from Rae Vending Machine.

1

Select a Product

Select a product directly from the screen, or Use the keypad to enter the slot code of the product you want, such as A1, B2 or C3.

2

Check Your Selection

The display will show the selected product, its price, and the quantity you want to buy. Use the + button to increase the quantity.

3

Proceed to Checkout

Click the Checkout button. The machine will display the total amount required for your selected product.

4

Enter Your Payment

Enter the amount you want to pay using the keypad, then click Pay. Your payment must cover the total price.

5

Collect Your Product

Your product will be dispensed. If you paid more than the total amount, your change will be displayed on the screen.
